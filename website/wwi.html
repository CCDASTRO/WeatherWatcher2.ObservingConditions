Observatory weather, rain protection and cloud advisories

WeatherWatcher & SafetyMonitor

Local RG-11 rain protection, NOAA satellite cloud information and optional Pushover warnings. Rain controls safety; clouds and notifications stay informational.

Rain protection stays local

The Arduino Uno reads the Hydreon RG-11 rain relay and isolated power monitor. Wet/rain, contact faults, power loss or lost serial communication prevent a SAFE result.

Existing automation uses UNSAFE to prohibit opening or close an open dome. A configurable dry-out delay defaults to five minutes. No Internet service is required for RG-11 protection.

NOAA replaces the IR sensor

The IR sky-temperature module has been removed. Windows reads the NOAA GOES-East ABI Level-2 Clear Sky Mask and calculates regional CloudCover around your observatory.

Defaults: 15 km radius, ten-minute polling and thirty-minute stale timeout. Cloudy conditions and failed or stale NOAA data do not change safety. Unavailable cloud data is reported unavailable, never zero.

Pushover cloud warnings

Optional phone/device advisories warn at 30% cloud cover and report clearing at 20%, with a configurable thirty-minute cooldown. Data-unavailable and recovered notices are optional.

Enter protected credentials under Cloud notifications... and send a labeled test. Alerts use normal priority and respect quiet hours. Delivery failures cannot delay rain handling or change SAFE/UNSAFE.

Keep your existing weather sources

Cumulus, Ambient Weather, weather limits and legacy Boltwood operation remain available. Arduino mode takes precedence over Boltwood; disabling it retains the existing file/API modes.

An independently selected Ambient API source retains its existing health checks. WeatherWatcher reports conditions and safety; your automation remains responsible for dome movement.

Set up the new workflow

  1. Close NINA and other ASCOM clients before installing matching drivers.
  2. Use the rain-only Uno sketch, remove the IR module and retain the RG-11 power/contact wiring.
  3. Enable Arduino/NOAA mode; configure the COM port, dry-out delay and observatory coordinates.
  4. Optionally configure Pushover credentials, thresholds and cooldown.
  5. Keep ObservingConditions connected and use the matching SafetyMonitor. Bench-test rain, connection loss, recovery and automation closure.
